The CAELUM **code** is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): you can use,
modify and redistribute it. The CAELUM **name and logo** are not covered by that license
(Apache 2.0, section 6). They are trademarks of **Issouf KANAZOE**, who created CAELUM
(and, later, of the company he creates to develop it). This policy explains how you may
use them.

The goal is simple: people must be able to tell the official CAELUM apart from
modified versions and from other products.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your software **uses**, **works with** or **is built on** CAELUM, for example
  "Powered by CAELUM" or "Compatible with CAELUM", as long as it is true.
- Name CAELUM in articles, tutorials, talks, comparisons and reviews.
- Redistribute the **unmodified** official package under its name.
- Use the name in a community group or meetup about CAELUM, if you make clear it is not
  run by the CAELUM maintainers.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use CAELUM (or a confusingly similar name such as "Caelum AI" or "Kaelum") as the name
  of your product, service, company, domain name or package.
- Distribute a **modified** version under the CAELUM name. Forks are welcome, but under
  another name; you may say "based on CAELUM".
- Offer a hosted service called CAELUM, or that suggests it is the official CAELUM
  Cloud.
- Use the logo, or suggest that the CAELUM maintainers endorse, sponsor or certify your
  product.

## Questions and permission requests

Open a [discussion](https://github.com/kanazoeissouf83-ops/caelum/discussions) or contact
the maintainers. This policy may evolve; the latest version is the one in this
repository.
